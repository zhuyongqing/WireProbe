Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 October 2026  ·  Version 1.0

In one sentence: the App does not collect or upload any of your data.

Your requests, responses and history stay on your own device. We do not run a server that receives them.

1. What we do not collect

The App contains no analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting or advertising SDK. It does not collect:

  • Your identity, contact details or location
  • Device identifiers such as IDFA or IDFV
  • The requests you send, the responses you receive, or your history
  • Usage behaviour, click streams or diagnostic logs

The App has no account system, so there is no registration data to collect in the first place.

2. Where your data lives

The following is stored only on your device, in a local database and the app's preferences. None of it is uploaded anywhere.

WhatDetails
Requests and collectionsThe requests, collections and environment variables you create
HistoryThe result of each request you send. To keep the store small, response bodies are truncated beyond 20,000 characters
Mock rulesThe simulated responses you configure
CredentialsBasic / Bearer / Digest / API key values you enter, kept only on this device
CookiesStored only if you turn on "Keep session" yourself

3. Where your requests go

The App sends requests directly to the address you type in. This path does not pass through us:

your device  ──►  the server you entered

We can see neither the request nor the response. Please make sure the destination is one you trust — in particular, any credentials you fill in are sent to that server along with the request.

4. Permissions

The App asks only for what it needs:

  • Local Network — required to reach an API server running on your own device or intranet, such as 192.168.1.5 or localhost.

The App does not request location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.

5. Third parties

The App integrates no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting service, and does not share data with anyone. If you install the App through TestFlight, Apple may collect crash and usage information under Apple's own privacy policy; that data does not reach us.

6. Deleting your data

  • Deleting a history entry or a collection inside the App removes it from the device immediately.
  • Deleting the App removes all of its local data with it.

Because we never hold your data, there is no separate "request deletion from the developer" step.

7. Children

The App is a tool intended for developers. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published inside the App together with a new effective date.
